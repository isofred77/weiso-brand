# WEISO — Brand

**The work shows in the iso.**

Brand files for [Weiso](https://shopweiso.com), basketball clothing for the hooper everyone on the court watches.

## What's here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `brand-book/` | Weiso Brand Book: PDF (to read and share with the team) and PowerPoint (to edit) |
| `logo/` | Main logo, the WI icon, as transparent PNGs in white and black |
| `creative-engine/` | The Creative Engine: brand and creative strategy workbook |

## Quick reference

- **Name:** WE + ISO. WE is the hooper everyone on the court watches: hard-working, tough, humble, focused. ISO is the 1v1, and the founder's nickname.
- **Slogan:** The work shows in the iso.
- **Main logo:** the WI icon. Full logo (icon + WEISO) and wordmark support it.
- **Colours:** black `#000000` and white `#FFFFFF`, with greys `#8C8C8C`, `#1A1A1A`, `#F2F2F0`. Products can be any colour.
- **Fonts:** Antonio (headlines), Inter (everything else). Both free on Google Fonts. Install them before editing the PowerPoint.

## Note

Moodboard photos in the brand book are reference images from other photographers. Internal use only, never post or use them in ads. Keep this repository private.
