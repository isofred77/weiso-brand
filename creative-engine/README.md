# Creative Engine

`Weiso_Creative_Engine.xlsx`: Weiso's creative strategy workbook (Nugen Founders template), filled in for Weiso.

## Tabs to start with

| Tab | What it is |
|---|---|
| Weiso · To Answer | Open questions only Fredrik can answer, most important first |
| Weiso · First Ad | Ready-to-film 30s founder ad ("They called me Iso") + 3 test hooks |
| Weiso · Margin | Profit per product and per order = the most we can pay for one sale in ads |
| Weiso · Shipping | Silkroad shipping model (country base fee + €/kg), checked against a real invoice |
| 01–15 | The template, filled in: brand core, buyers, awareness, angles, plays, 30 hooks, casting, proof, objections, test plan |

## Reading it

- Dark text = filled in. Copper italic starting with "▸ YOU:" = still needs your input.
- In Margin and Shipping, blue numbers on yellow are inputs you can change; everything else recalculates.

## Key numbers (Oct 2026)

- Hero product: Black Goat Shorts (#1 by revenue, last 12 months).
- Profit per order, after product cost, VAT, fees and shipping: ~€17 single shorts, ~€27 for buy-2-get-1 regular shorts, ~€18 for buy-2-get-1 reversibles.
- Shipping: ~€11.50 per average order; customers pay ~€4–5.

Customer names from the shipping invoice are removed; parcels are matched by order number only.
